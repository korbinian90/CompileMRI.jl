Example data for the Matlab scripts next to this folder.

Phase.nii and Mag.nii are a small three echo dataset, 51x51x41x3, the same one
the test suite runs against. The echo times used in the examples, [1 2 3],
belong to this data.

They are here so example_ROMEO_call.m and example_CLEARSWI_call.m run as
downloaded. Replace the two filenames at the top of those scripts with your own
data once you have seen them work.
